## Decisions

Technical defaults of the stage-0 author unless marked. No owner question was asked (marathon rule); the UX forks carry a Ruling (six). Revision 2 rewrote decisions 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10 in place and added 11, 12 and 13 (no renumbering); revision 3 amended 1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12 and 13 in place; `## Review rounds` maps every review finding to its fix.

1. **The chip does not live under a backdrop (Ruling 1).** While any modal layer is open (a `.desktop-dialog`, the city modal in either mode, the onboarding wizard) the chip is not painted: its rectangle shows what the backdrop shows. It is not removed from the accessibility tree: no `display: none`, no `hidden` attribute set by this feature, no `visibility: hidden`, and no `inert` (it is outside `#favorites`, and `inert` would remove it from the tree); it keeps its layout box, so its size stays measurable and the reserve (decision 12) does not move. Mechanism is the plan's (for example `opacity: 0; pointer-events: none` under the selector of decision 11). Its text, its `hidden` state and its timer are not touched while it is covered: `DESKTOP_STATUS_MS` runs on, the timer is neither restarted nor extended, and the chip is painted again when the last layer closes unless its timer or the next action cleared it meanwhile. The z-index stays 50 and the chip is never raised above a backdrop. The Reload button exists only in the layout-restore failure chip (`showLayoutRestoreFailure`); it is not reachable by the Tab walk inside a layer (the Tab trap of the wizard, the city modal and the dialog wraps within the layer, and a first Tab from outside it, for example from the address bar, is redirected to the layer's first control, the `keydown` handler on `trapRoot` in `newtab.js`) and not by pointer (the backdrop covers it). This is pinned by a source test of the handler, not by an E2E case (AS-LLW-11). Not claimed: that a screen reader reads a message that arrives while an `aria-modal` layer is open. `#desktop-status` is outside the dialog, as it is today; this is an assistive-technology check outside E2E (Accepted exceptions). Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): the status is hidden while a modal is open; альтернатива: raise the chip above the backdrop (z 103) and keep it visible, or move the dialog out of its way; цена ошибки: the ensure-failure message of a first run is not seen until the wizard is closed (it is persistent, so it is there afterwards), with the alternative the chip covers a dialog button in windows lower than about 360 px and the dialog needs a bottom reserve.
2. **A visible chip takes room (Ruling 2).** While the chip exists (its `hidden` attribute is not set), in normal mode and in edit mode (a failed drop shows it in edit mode, `showDesktopStatus(message)` in the drag failure path), the page keeps a bottom reserve equal to the distance from the window bottom to the chip's top (the chip's height plus `--status-chip-offset-bottom`), so the last row of a scrolled grid can be scrolled clear of the chip and a focused tile is scrolled to above the chip. The reserve belongs to the existence of the chip, not to its being painted: a covered chip keeps it, so a layer opening or closing never changes the document height or moves `scrollY` (decision 11). Mechanics, the limits (no layout input is touched, no new scroll on a desk that fits, the jump when the chip goes away) and the resize rule are decision 12. Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): reserve room instead of letting the chip float over tiles; альтернатива: leave the chip floating (a transient 8 s message) or dock it at the top; цена ошибки: the page grows by about 60 px at the bottom while a message is shown (a one-time jump of the scroll range, the scroll position itself does not move), with the alternative a tile under the chip cannot be read or clicked while a persistent message (the Reload chip) is shown.
3. **A stacked modal hides the card under it (Ruling 3).** While the change-mode city modal is stacked over the weather edit dialog, the lower dialog is not painted: `.desktop-dialog[inert] { visibility: hidden }`. `inert` is set on a `.desktop-dialog` only by `showCityModal` when it stacks (`desktopDialogRoot.inert = true`, `newtab.js`) and cleared only by `hideCityModal` (`desktopDialogRoot.inert = false`); `#favorites` is the other `inert` target and is not a dialog. So no new class or flag is added and no path can leave the dialog hidden: removing `inert` repaints it in the same style recalculation that precedes `applyPendingFocus`, so focus can land on its city field (checked by AS-LLW-03). The dialog stays in the DOM with its form values; the desktop backdrop stays, so the dimming does not change. Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): hide the lower card while the stacked modal is open; альтернатива: keep the lower card visible as context and cover its strips (a taller/wider upper card or a second backdrop); цена ошибки: the user loses the visual context of the weather dialog for the seconds the city modal is open, with the alternative strips stay visible in the corners and the cards fight for attention.
4. **Ring room in every scrolling dialog.** Top and sides: `scroll-margin: 5px` on `.desktop-dialog :is(button, input)`, a literal with the same reason as the city modal's rule (the widest ring is the forced-colors outline, 2px + 2px offset = 4px, plus one pixel for Chrome's integer scroll offsets); on the resting state because the scroll happens at the moment of focus. The rings are `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px` (fields, buttons, the colour input, the `.segmented` group; `surfaces.css`), 4 px of reach in forced colors, not a 3 px ring. The segmented controls and the size radiogroup are a special case: the ring is painted on `.segmented:has(input:focus-visible)` while the focused `input` is a 1×1 clipped box inside an option (`controls.css`, `.segmented__option input`), so a `scroll-margin` on the input does not reach the group's ring. The observable is that the group's ring box (measured on `.segmented`) is whole and above the footer; the mechanism is the plan's (a margin on the input large enough to reach the group edge, or a `focusin` handler that scrolls the `.segmented` into view with `block: "nearest"`). Bottom edge: a sticky footer covers the lower part of the scrollport, so room above it comes from `scroll-padding-bottom` on `.desktop-dialog` (decision 13), which `focus()` and `scrollIntoView` honour. Add link, Edit link, Edit weather and the two confirm dialogs get the rules; the confirm dialogs and Edit weather do not scroll at the windows measured so far and are control cases (AS-LLW-04).
5. **The footer is sticky (Ruling 4).** In a `.desktop-dialog` that scrolls, the `Cancel | Save` row stays at the bottom edge of the card (`position: sticky; bottom: 0`, `--panel` background that covers the scrolling content, a divider above it). The geometry (which element, the padding recipe, the divider, `scroll-padding-bottom`) is decision 13. A dialog that fits (1280×800) has no scrolling and is pixel-identical to `origin/main` (±0.5 px for every rectangle). The footer keeps the 50/50 buttons, their order in the DOM, the focus order and the 16 px gap above the buttons; the error slot stays above the footer in the flow. Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): sticky footer; альтернатива: a scroll hint (fade/shadow at the bottom) and keep the buttons in the flow, or a larger min viewport for dialogs; цена ошибки: in a very low window (≤ 300 px) the sticky row takes a third of the card and the form scrolls in a small window, with the alternative the user has to discover that the buttons are below (the original defect). The rejected alternative "sticky row" of `docs/city-modal-low-window.md` applied to the first-run reserve; this decision is about desktop dialogs only and does not touch the city modal.
6. **A validation error is shown, then the field (Ruling 5).** `showDialogError` still focuses the named field (or Save), now with `preventScroll: true`, then scrolls the dialog instantly (`behavior: "auto"`, never smooth): the visible height is the scrollport minus the sticky footer (the `scroll-padding-bottom` of decision 13). If the field's top and the slot's bottom (with their 5 px margins) fit in that visible height together, the dialog scrolls so that both are above the footer's top edge; otherwise the slot wins: it is scrolled to just above the footer and the focused field may be partly or wholly out of view (1280×300, where they are ~318 px apart). The confirm dialogs carry the same slot but never receive an error: Delete and Hide call `runDesktopMutation` without `dialogRoot`, so a failure goes to the page chip and the dialog closes (AS-LLW-13e). `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, the focus target and the text are unchanged. A later write error with `preventScroll` does not get `scroll-margin` protection for the ring (Accepted exceptions). Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): the message wins over the field when both do not fit; альтернатива: the field wins (today's behaviour: the message needs a scroll); цена ошибки: a sighted user may not see the field they must fix (the message names it, and focus is on it), with the alternative they do not see that anything failed.
7. **Closing a dialog does not scroll the page by itself; a target that a write moved or removed is brought in by the nearest edge (technical).** `focusWidgetTarget` has four other callers (the weather metric restore, `closeAddMenu`, `recoverFromGoneLink`, the delete/hide neighbour), so `preventScroll` is not its default. `closeDesktopDialog` has two modes. (a) A close without a write that moves anything (Cancel, Escape, backdrop, a failed write, a failed Delete or Hide) restores focus to the opener with `preventScroll: true` and reveals nothing: the page returns to the state it had before the dialog opened. (b) A close after a successful write that can move or remove the focus target (Save in Edit link and in Edit weather, which can change `w`/`h` and repack the grid; a successful Delete or Hide, whose target is the neighbour or the fallback tile; `recoverFromGoneLink` with its neighbours) focuses with `preventScroll: true` and then calls `scrollIntoView({ block: "nearest", behavior: "auto" })` on the target only when it is not safely inside the window (the 6 px of `scroll-margin`, and above the chip reserve, which `scroll-padding-bottom` of the root already encodes): at most the minimum scroll, instant, never through the top of the page. Today's `focus()` revealed the tile after a Save; mode (b) keeps that result without the jump of the opener case. A new tile after Add is still revealed only by `revealAddedTile` (smooth, only if not safely inside the window, `docs/link-dialog-validation.md`); its "safely inside" bottom edge is the window height minus the chip reserve when a chip is shown. The caller passes the mode (an option of `closeDesktopDialog`); the plan names it.
8. **Badges clear the grid edge and the neighbours at the 56 cell.** `--tile-remove-offset` becomes 6px at `:root[data-cell="56"]` (gap 6, pad 8): the badge sits 6 px outside the grid's top and left edge, which is 2 px below the document's top edge (page padding 8) and no longer at 0, and it touches but does not enter a neighbour. The reference for the measurement is the document (top) and the neighbour tiles, not `.desktop-grid`, whose frame the badge leaves by design (`left`/`top` are `… - var(--tile-remove-offset)`). Cells 64 and 72 keep 8px (gap 8: touches the neighbour; pad 12/16).
9. **One ring over content (Ruling 6).** The tokens: `--focus-ring` (solid) is the ring of a non-control target that takes focus programmatically or as a keyboard scroll stop (the busy title with `tabindex=-1`, the locked starter list); `--focus-overlay-ring` (translucent) stays for the rings of controls drawn on the plain panel (`box-shadow` of fields and buttons in the city modal, the link). The title is on the plain panel (step 1 measured it against `--panel`, 4.12:1 and 6.17:1, not a contrast defect), so the choice of the solid token for it is a consistency judgement, not a contrast fix, and it is the reason for Ruling 6. The title ring is `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--focus-ring)`. The locked starter list already has `outline: 2px solid var(--focus-ring); outline-offset: -2px` (`surfaces.css`, `[data-starters-locked="true"]:focus-visible`), so the defect is the paint order, not the token: the opaque rows cover the list's own inset outline (step 1: the ring shows in the gaps and at the corners only; the rows are `position: static`). The fix paints the ring where the rows cannot cover it, and the ring stays fixed to the list's box: the list scrolls (`overflow-y: auto`, capped height), so a pseudo-element inside it would scroll away with the content; the observable is a 2 px ring of one colour along the whole box of the list, at `scrollTop = 0` and at `scrollTop > 0`, ≥ 3:1 on `--panel` and on the row surface in both themes. The mechanism is the plan's (for example an outline outside the list's box, or an overlay that does not scroll). Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): solid ring token over content, which makes the title ring darker than before; альтернатива: keep the soft ring and add a light ring around it for content; цена ошибки: a slightly harsher title focus ring in the busy phase, with the alternative a double ring that is harder to maintain.
10. **Forced-colors ring of the location link (technical).** The rule `.city-location-link:focus-visible { outline-offset: 0 }` would lose: `.city-modal :is(button, input, .weather-form__suggestion):focus-visible` has specificity (0,3,0) (`:is` takes its most specific argument, `.weather-form__suggestion`) and sets `outline-offset: 2px`, against (0,2,0) for the link's own selector. The rule is therefore `.city-modal .city-location-link:focus-visible { outline-offset: 0 }` ((0,3,0)), placed after the shared rule in `surfaces.css` so that the equal specificity is decided by order. The forced-colors outline then hugs the padded box (1 px padding past the text) and the ring reaches 3 px, inside the 3.38 px gap before the link measured at step 1, so it does not enter the last letter of the text before the link. The wizard's link is inside a `.city-modal` root as well.
11. **The covered state is derived from the DOM (technical).** "A layer is open" is the selector `body:has(.desktop-dialog, .city-modal)` (the wizard root is a `.city-modal`, `.onboarding-wizard.city-modal`), not a flag set on each open and close path. A path that forgets to clear a flag (an error, a teardown, a stale tab) cannot leave the chip hidden or showing wrongly, and no state is added to `weatherUiState.js` or `desktopUiState.js`. Consequences, each an AS: (a) a chip set while a layer is open (a retry failing late; a write failure) exists and is covered, and is painted when the layer closes if its 8 s, counted from when it was set, have not passed; (b) closing the city modal stacked over the weather dialog leaves the chip covered, because the dialog is still in the DOM; (c) a chip whose timer ran out while covered is `hidden` and is not painted after the close; (d) a retry whose success arrives while a layer is open does not create a chip and says `Weather updated` in `#desktop-live` as today; (e) a failed Delete or Hide from a confirm dialog sets the chip and closes the dialog in the same task (`confirmDeleteFavorite`), so the chip is painted at once and the opener is focused.
12. **Reserve mechanics (technical).** A custom property `--status-reserve` on `:root`, in px, written from the chip: a `ResizeObserver` on `#desktop-status` writes `height + --status-chip-offset-bottom`, where the height is the border box (`entry.borderBoxSize[0].blockSize`, or `offsetHeight`; not `contentRect`, which leaves out the chip's padding and border) (the chip is `position: fixed`, so this is the distance from the window bottom to its top); the observer fires when `hidden` toggles (size 0, reserve 0), when the text or the Reload row changes the height, and when a resize re-wraps it (the height of one message is 41.59 px at 1280 and 61.19 px at 500 and 320; the ensure failure 61.19, 80.78 and 100.38 px). It is used twice: `.desktop { padding-bottom: calc(var(--grid-pad, 16px) + var(--status-reserve, 0px)) }` (document height only; `.desktop` is `box-sizing: border-box; min-height: 100vh`, so a desk that fits with room to spare does not grow) and `html { scroll-padding-bottom: var(--status-reserve, 0px) }` (a tile focused or scrolled into view stops above the chip; the tile's own `scroll-margin: 6px` adds to it). Limits: (1) nothing the layout reads changes: `viewportRows`, the column count, `firstScreen()` and tile positions read `clientHeight`/`clientWidth`, which the reserve does not touch, and nothing is written to storage; (2) a desk that fits but ends within the reserve of the window bottom starts to scroll by at most the reserve while the chip exists, so the last row can be scrolled clear; the classic-scrollbar concern (a scrollbar changing `clientWidth` and the column count) does not arise, because `html { scrollbar-gutter: stable }` already reserves the gutter (`newtab.css`), and AS-LLW-02 asserts `clientWidth` unchanged; (3) when the chip goes away at the end of the page the browser clamps `scrollY` to the new, shorter range and the content moves down by up to the reserve (about 60 px). This is accepted (Accepted exceptions); AS-LLW-02 asserts that `scrollY` equals the new maximum and that nothing else moves.
13. **Sticky-footer geometry (technical).** The row is `.favorite-form__footer` (`createDialogFooter` and the form footer of Add/Edit link): in Add link, Edit link and Edit weather it is the last child of the form (a flex column), in the two confirm dialogs it is a direct child of `.desktop-dialog`, the scroll container. The selector is `.desktop-dialog .favorite-form__footer`. A sticky `bottom: 0` is measured from the padding box of the scroll container, so it would leave the card's 20 px bottom padding empty only at the end of the scroll and show content in it otherwise; the recipe is `padding-bottom: var(--surface-modal-padding); margin-bottom: calc(-1 * var(--surface-modal-padding))` (the footer's box reaches the card's bottom edge, its background hides the content below the buttons, and the layout height is unchanged), and the 16 px gap above the buttons moves from `margin-top: var(--modal-actions-margin-top)` to an equal padding-top on the footer (the same position at rest, now covered by the footer's background; the divider takes 1 px of it). Therefore the distance from the buttons' bottom edge to the card's padding-box bottom edge is 20 px, at rest and while stuck (±0.5 px); the card has a 1 px border (`--control-border-width`), so to the outer edge (`getBoundingClientRect().bottom`) it is 21 px, as on `origin/main`. The 16 px gap above the buttons is the distance from the bottom of the content above (the error slot, or the last row) to the buttons' top edge, equal to `origin/main` at rest (±0.5); the footer's own top edge is 1 px higher (the divider) and covers that gap. `scroll-padding-bottom` on `.desktop-dialog` is the footer's full height: `calc(var(--modal-actions-margin-top) + var(--control-height) + var(--surface-modal-padding))`, and AS-LLW-05 asserts the computed value against the measured footer height (±0.5 px); the footer stays one row at every width. The divider is a real 1 px `border-top` in `--border` (a `box-shadow` or a background disappears in forced colors), with the footer's padding-top reduced by 1 px so the height is unchanged; it is painted only when the dialog scrolls and absent when it fits (pixel row equal to `--panel`), so a fitting dialog is unchanged. Mechanism is the plan's, for example `data-scrolls="true"` on the dialog kept equal to `scrollHeight > clientHeight` by a `ResizeObserver` on the dialog and its form, which covers every event that changes the height without a list: open, window resize and zoom, the error slot showing or hiding, the Icon mode switch (the Custom icon row is hidden until Custom is chosen, so Favicon ↔ Custom crosses the threshold at 320×600), a font load. AS-LLW-05 asserts the transition both ways. The footer buttons are sticky themselves: moving focus from Cancel to Save does not change `scrollTop`; focusing Cancel from the last field may scroll the dialog to its end (accepted). The footer has `z-index: 1` so no positioned field paints over it.

- Then: the focused tile (the edited tile, or the neighbour in case 3) lies fully inside the window with at least 6 px (and above the chip reserve when a chip is shown), `scrollY` grew by at most its overhang plus 6 px (+1) in one step (no glide, no frame above the starting `scrollY`); Cancel: `scrollY` unchanged (±1); a Save on a tile in view: `scrollY` unchanged (±1).

## Accepted exceptions

- Ruling 1: a status shown while a modal is open is not seen until it closes; a screen reader may not read a message that arrives while an `aria-modal` layer is open (the chip is outside the dialog, as today); not checked by E2E.
- Decision 12: when the chip goes away at the end of the page the content moves down by up to the reserve (about 60 px) once.
- Ruling 3: the weather dialog is not visible behind the stacked city modal.
- Ruling 4: at heights ≤ 300 px the sticky row takes a large part of the card; focusing Cancel from the last field may scroll the dialog to its end.
- Ruling 4 (low end): windows below 1280×240 (400 % zoom, 320×200) are not measured; the footer then takes about 76 of 168 px and the form scrolls in the rest; the cost is accepted for the same reason as at 300 px.
- Ruling 5: at 1280×300 the field may be out of view when the message is shown.
- Decision 7: after a `preventScroll` focus (the opener, the field of `showDialogError`) the ring has no `scroll-margin` protection: if the opener or the field was already partly outside the window or the scrollport when the user left it, its ring stays partly cut; nothing scrolls on the user's behalf.
