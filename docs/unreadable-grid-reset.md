## Terms

- **Unreadable**: the stored widgets meta is `invalid` in the sense of `inspectWidgetsMeta` (`src/widgetsStore.js`): present and neither a valid version 3 meta nor a meta of a newer version. That includes a malformed meta, a value that is not an object, and a version 1 or 2 meta of an older build (decision 3 of `docs/remove-migrations.md`). `newer` is not unreadable: it is a rollback case and keeps its own message.
- **The screen**: the page content for an unreadable meta (decision 4): one line of text and one button.
- **Reset**: removing the widgets meta and every `quietTabWidget:<id>` key from `chrome.storage.sync`, then loading the page again so that the normal first load runs (decision 6).
- **The default block**: the six system tiles, `b` rows high (`defaultBlockRows`: 1 row where 8 columns fit, 2 rows in a window narrower than 664 px); **the island**: the six tiles plus the starter links after the onboarding Finish (`docs/post-onboarding-grid.md`), `H` rows high (3 to 5); **the first screen**: `viewportRows(clientHeight, clientWidth)` rows, written `R` (`docs/vertically-centered-defaults.md` decision 2); **the reference window**: 1280×800, `viewportRows(800, 1280) = 9` rows (`CEILING_REFERENCE_ROWS`, decision 2).
- **L1-02**: the open finding of the run 40 final design review (lens 1) that the page for an unreadable meta says nothing; it became the accepted exception of `docs/remove-migrations.md` and is the second half of backlog item 95.

## Decisions

1. **What the run does.** Three independent changes of the same page boot, in the order of the tasks of the plan: (a) the ceiling for the first block (decision 2, item 43); (b) the removal of the `tileSize` tolerance (decision 3, item 94); (c) the screen for an unreadable meta with Reset grid (decisions 4 to 8, items L1-02 and 95). Item 44 changes nothing (Accepted exception 1).

2. **The ceiling for the first block (item 43).** `centeredDefaultRow(rows, blockRows)` returns `min(cap, max(0, floor((rows - blockRows) / 2)))` with `cap = max(0, floor((CEILING_REFERENCE_ROWS - blockRows) / 2))` and a new exported constant `CEILING_REFERENCE_ROWS = 9` in `src/desktopLayout.js`: **9 is the number of rows of a 1280×800 window** (`viewportRows(800, 1280)`; a unit test pins the equality, so the constant cannot drift from its meaning). So a block never stands lower than the row at which it would be centered in a 1280×800 window. The same value is `max(0, floor((min(rows, 9) - blockRows) / 2))` (the window counts as at most 9 rows high; a unit test checks that the two forms agree for `rows` 1 to 80 and `blockRows` 1 to 12). The cap by block height `b`: 4 for one row, 3 for two or three, 2 for four or five, 1 for six or seven, 0 from eight. Consequences, all by that one function, which both callers share:
   - the default block of a fresh install, a heal of a cleared storage (`healClearedDesk` reads the first screen again) and the first placement by `placeMissing` with all six absent take at most **row 4 (a one-row block) or row 3 (the two-row block of a window narrower than 664 px, rows 3 and 4)**. The cap bites from `R = 11` for a block of odd height and from `R = 10` for a block of even height (`## Live measurement` gives the first height per width). At width 1280 `Y0` = 4, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1 at 800, 700, 600, 500, 400, 300 and 4 at 903 px (all as today), and capped from 904 px (`R = 11`; today 5, 6, 8, 12 at 904 or 960, 1080, 1440, 2160); in a narrow window 500×735 and 320×629 (`R = 9`) keep rows 3 and 4 as today, while 500×736, 500×800 and 320×630 (`R = 10`; today row 4) and 500×900 and 320×800 (today 5) take row 3;
   - the island of the onboarding Finish (`islandPlacements`, `dy = centeredDefaultRow(rows, H)`) is capped by its own height: an island of 3 rows (k = 0, 1, 2) at most row 3, of 4 or 5 rows (k = 3 to 8) at most row 2. It therefore ends at row 5 (`H` 3 or 4) or row 6 (`H` 5) at the latest, and Settings and Add, which sit in the last row of every island, are fully inside the first screen of every window from 504 px (k = 0 to 5, 8) or 584 px (k = 6, 7) at width 1280 (the reference window shows every island whole);
   - the default block is fully inside the first screen of every window that has at least 5 rows (424 px at 601 and wider, 376 px at 361 to 600, 320 px up to 360), an island of 3 or 4 rows from 6 rows (504 px at 1280) and of 5 rows from 7 rows (584 px); a window that is lower still shows it by scrolling, as for any tile that sits below the fold (Accepted exception 7).
   Nothing else moves: the partial self-heal (1 to 5 ids) keeps the row-0 rule, `placeNew` and a restored metric keep the first row with a free cell, a stored `y` is never recomputed (decision 6 of `docs/vertically-centered-defaults.md`), a block already stored at row 5 or lower stays where it is. `placeMissing` takes the target row only as the place to start its search by distance (above first): the rows of a fresh install and of a heal of a cleared storage are free, so "at most row 4" is exact there; with other tiles in the way the nearest free row is taken, as before.
   Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): the ceiling is the row at which a block of that height would be centered in a 1280×800 window: 4 for the default block in one row, 3 for the two-row block of a narrow window, 3 or 2 for the island (3 rows, 4 or 5 rows), and it applies to the island of the onboarding Finish too; альтернатива: the constant row 4 for every height (revision 1: an island of 3 rows would stand one row lower than centered at 1280×800, of 4 or 5 rows two rows lower, so Settings and Add of the island would be fully inside the first screen only from 584, 664 or 744 px instead of 504, 504 or 584 px, and the default wizard choice of three starter links (k = 3) would show them only from 664 px), or no ceiling for the island (the island stays centered in a tall window and has the same Chrome Sync problem); цена ошибки: the two-row block of a narrow window stands higher than today, which Kier did not ask for in so many words (his words are about the default block, about row 4): 500×800 takes row 3 instead of 4, and 360×800, 320×800 and 500×900 row 3 instead of 5; with the constant the island would instead stand one or two rows lower than centered at 1280×800 (above).

3. **No legacy `tileSize` (item 94).** A stored `tileSize` is ignored data, like the keys of `docs/remove-migrations.md` decision 2: not read, not validated, not written, not removed. Removed: `isOptionalTileSize` and its two uses and the comment above `isWeatherMetricItem` (`src/widgetsStore.js`), the `TILE_SIZES` import there and the export in `src/favoritesShared.js`, the `tileSize` branch of `sizeOf`, the two `delete ….tileSize` lines of `src/widgetsService.js`. Consequences, all for an item that carries the field: a value that is not `square` or `wide` (`"huge"`) no longer makes an item invalid (it is read like any item); an item without a valid grid and with `tileSize: "wide"` is no longer 2×1 by that field. **`sizeOf` replaces the branch by the default size of the item**: its valid grid, else a weather metric's `defaultSize(id)` (2×1 for precipitation and air quality, 1×1 for temperature and UV), else 1×1. Reason: a metric has one default footprint everywhere else (`ensureWidgetsLayout`, the restore from Add, `placeholderGrid` in `src/widgetsService.js`), so a metric whose cell is broken keeps the size it has on a normal desk, and what `dg-28` shows today (the broken precipitation tile at 2×1) stays true without the legacy field. The seeds of the harness that carry `tileSize` lose it: `legacyFavorite` (default `tileSize: "square"`), `metric()` and `DEFAULT_SIZES` (they feed the old-data seeds of `dg-81` and `dg-14`, which are compared byte for byte and never read as a layout, so the shape need not be period-exact); `favV2` stops stripping it; `dg-28` drops `{ tileSize: "wide" }` from its metric (the broken grid `w: 5` stays: the tile is still unplaced and, by `defaultSize`, still 2×1, so its expected cell and the dialog's selected size do not change); `09-weather-ensure` AS-2 partial drops the field from its orphan seed and checks `enabled === false` and an unchanged item instead. Unit tests that pin the tolerance are rewritten (`## Affected earlier scenarios`).

4. **The screen (items L1-02, 95).** When the boot finds an unreadable meta, the page draws the screen instead of an empty grid, and does nothing else.
   - **When.** In the bootstrap of `newtab.js`, after the early `newer` check (which still wins) and before `ensureWidgetsLayout`: `inspectWidgetsMeta(rawMeta) === "invalid"` sets a new flag `widgetsUnreadable`, calls `renderFavorites()` and returns, the same shape as the `newer` lock. The early read may fail (`rawMeta = {}`, decision 6 of `docs/remove-migrations.md`); then the meta is read again by `ensureWidgetsLayout`, which already reports it: a result with `meta === "invalid"` takes the same exit (flag, render, return) before `getState()`. `widgetsState` stays `null`, so the tab-return re-read has nothing to refresh (`deskRefreshDecision` says `skip`), the first-run wizard cannot open (no enabled metric) and nothing is fetched (no `startWeather`), exactly as for `newer`. Nothing is written by the boot (zero `sync.set`, zero `sync.remove`).
   - **What.** `renderDesktop` gets a branch after `widgetsNewer` and before `!widgetsState`: `favoritesRoot.replaceChildren(block)` where `block` is a `div.grid-unreadable` holding (1) `createStatus("Your grid couldn't be read.", { error: true, live: "assertive", full: true })` (a `p.status.status--error.status--full[role=alert][aria-live=assertive]`, the same element and the same danger color as the newer-version message) and (2) `button.button[type=button][data-grid-action="reset"]` with the `refresh` icon and the visible text **Reset grid** (accessible name `Reset grid`). No `.desktop-grid`, no tile, no Settings or Add, no other text. `#favorites` keeps `aria-label="Grid"` and `data-edit="false"`.
   - **Where (Ruling).** The block is centered in the window horizontally and vertically (`display: flex; flex-direction: column; align-items: center; justify-content: center; gap: var(--space-grid-12)` inside `.desktop`, `min-height` the window minus the desk padding, text centered; `.grid-unreadable .button { width: auto }` so that the shared "full width below 601 px" rule does not stretch it; `.grid-unreadable .button:focus-visible { background: var(--soft-fill-strong); box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--focus-overlay-ring); outline-color: transparent; }`, the same three declarations as `.desktop-status__reload:focus-visible`, because the plain `.button` ring (3 px `--focus`) is 1.78:1 in light and 2.52:1 in dark against this page background, below the 3:1 of a focus indicator, and the soft ring is 3.99:1 and 6.59:1 (Live measurement; the rule of `docs/design-system.md` "Overlays" and the existing `test/focusTokens.test.js`, which already requires a `box-shadow` ring beside every transparent outline). At 1280×800 the block center is the window center (400 px) and the page does not scroll; a window lower than the block and the desk padding (under about 104 px) scrolls like any page. While the failure chip of decision 6 is visible the page scrolls by the chip's `--status-reserve` (the shared mechanism of every chip, Live measurement: 58 px at 1280×800) and the block does not move; the screen does not subtract the reserve from its `min-height` and does not change that mechanism. Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): the screen is a centered block; альтернатива: top left under the desk padding like the newer-version message (a status line with a button below it); цена ошибки: with the centered block a change of mind costs one CSS rule; top left would leave a destructive button in a corner of an otherwise blank window, farther from where a person looks.
   - **Not for `newer`.** A meta of a newer version keeps its text, its read-only lock and no button (a newer build wrote it and this build must not erase it; the answer is to update, as the text says). `docs/remove-migrations.md` decision 3 stays.
   - **The advice of `INVALID_WIDGETS_MESSAGE`** ("Your saved grid could not be read, so changes are paused. Reload this tab; if this keeps happening, update Quiet Tab.") is not changed: the reload now leads to the screen, which is what makes the advice true (item 95).

5. **The confirmation.** A click or an activation of the screen's button opens a desktop dialog of a new kind `confirm-reset-grid` (added to `DIALOG_KINDS`; opened through `openDesktopDialog`, so `#favorites` is inert, the backdrop, the focus trap, Escape and the backdrop rules of every confirm dialog hold) with:
   - title `Reset grid?` (`h2.desktop-dialog__title`), body `Your links will be removed.` (`p.desktop-dialog__body`, `aria-describedby` of the dialog), the shared hidden error slot that every confirm dialog builds (`createDialogErrorSlot()`; it stays hidden: the failure of the removal goes to `#desktop-status`, decision 6);
   - the footer, two 50/50 buttons: **Cancel** (`x` icon, `data-favorite-action="cancel"`) and **Reset grid** (`button.button--danger`, `refresh` icon, `data-dialog-action="reset-grid"`); both labels stay on one line at every width: one rule, `.desktop-dialog[data-dialog="confirm-reset-grid"] .favorite-form__footer .button { white-space: nowrap; }` (no new breakpoint; it matters only below 328 px, where `Reset grid`, 123 px wide with its icon, no longer fits its half: at 320 px the buttons are 125 and 113 px instead of 119 and 119, Accepted exception 14);
   - focus on opening: Cancel (the first enabled button, as in every confirm dialog);
   - Cancel, Escape and a backdrop click (after the existing 300 ms backdrop guard) close it, write nothing, return focus to the screen's Reset grid button (looked up by `data-grid-action="reset"` at close time: a render may have replaced the node) and call `armPostCloseClickGuard()` (below);
   - **A double click must neither confirm nor flip the dialog.** The dialog is centered like the screen's block, so the lower right part of the screen button lies under the dialog's own Reset grid button and its lower left part under Cancel (Live measurement). Four rules, on this dialog kind only:
     (a) for the first 300 ms after the dialog opened (`DIALOG_BACKDROP_GUARD_MS`, the value the backdrop already uses) a **pointer** click (`event.detail > 0`) on either of its two buttons does nothing; a keyboard activation (`detail` 0) is not delayed;
     (b) at any time a click with `event.detail > 1` on either of its two buttons does nothing (a slow double click: the interval of the operating system is longer than 300 ms; the first click of a double click has `detail` 1, so a deliberate single click keeps working);
     (c) in the cases (a) and (b) the `mousedown` of the press is cancelled by default (`preventDefault`), so the press of the second click does not move the focus from Cancel to the danger button, where a later Enter or Space (`detail` 0, never delayed) would confirm: the pattern of the capture `mousedown` listener next to `armPostCloseClickGuard` in `src/newtab.js` (backlog 99);
     (d) when the dialog is closed by Cancel, Escape or the backdrop, `armPostCloseClickGuard()` runs: the second click of a double click on Cancel lands on the screen's button, which sits under it, and would open the dialog again.
     After 300 ms a single click (`detail` 1) on either button is a decision.
   - **While the removal runs** (decision 6) the dialog carries `aria-busy="true"` and ignores Cancel, Reset grid, Escape and the backdrop (the existing `favoritesBusy` rule); no visible busy look (the delete confirm has none) and no timer (decision 7, Accepted exception 13). A press on the backdrop moves the focus to `body` as on any dialog (the backdrop is not focusable); the focus trap brings it back on the next Tab.

6. **The reset.** A new function `resetUnreadableWidgets(storageArea)` in `src/widgetsStore.js`, next to `ensureWidgetsLayout`, runs under the same extension-wide lock (`withWidgetsMutationLock`):
   - it reads the area **once**, `const snapshot = await storageArea.get(null)`, and takes both the meta check and the key list from that one object: `inspectWidgetsMeta(snapshot)` (a `get(null)` result has the same shape as the result of reading the meta key, and holds that key) and the keys that start with `quietTabWidget:`, so the two cannot disagree (a second read could see a readable meta that Chrome Sync brought in between and list the keys of a readable grid). **Only `invalid` is erased.** For `valid`, `missing` or `newer` it removes nothing and returns `{ reset: false, meta: <kind> }`: another tab already reset or fixed the data, and a Reset grid pressed on an outdated screen must never erase a grid that is readable now;
   - for `invalid` it calls `remove` **once** with `quietTabWidgetsMeta` and every key of the snapshot that starts with `quietTabWidget:` (the item keys that the unreadable meta does not list included; with an unreadable meta `order` cannot be trusted, and a leftover item key would come back through `ensureWidgetsLayout`, which reads `quietTabWidget:<system id>` directly), and returns `{ reset: true, meta: "invalid" }`. A failing `get` rejects before any `remove`; a failing `remove` rejects and removes nothing (one call at most). The one window that stays open is between that `get(null)` and the `remove` (Accepted exception 2);
   - **nothing else is erased**: the city (`quietTabWeatherLocation`), the weather cache, the onboarding flags (`local`), the legacy favorites keys (`docs/remove-migrations.md` decision 2) and every other key stay as they are.
   The page's handler (`resetGrid`, started by the dialog's Reset grid button; it begins with `if (favoritesBusy) return;` as `confirmDeleteFavorite` does): `startFavoritesAction({ render: false })` (busy), `await resetUnreadableWidgets(syncStorageArea)`; **on any result (`reset: true` or `false`) the page reloads (`location.reload()`)**: the normal first load then runs (`ensureWidgetsLayout` for the first screen of the new window, the weather, the first-run decision), which is what "start a fresh install" means, and for `reset: false` it shows whatever is stored now. On a rejection (of the `get` or of the `remove`): `finishFavoritesAction`, the dialog closes (focus to the screen's button, no scroll), `#desktop-status` shows **`Couldn't reset your grid. Try again in a moment.`** (the page-level failure place, as for the delete confirm; cleared by the next action or after 8 s, `DESKTOP_STATUS_MS`), and the screen stays; a second attempt is allowed at once.
   Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): the reset keeps the weather city and the onboarding flags, so a person who already onboarded gets the six default tiles with the stored city back, and a profile that never finished onboarding gets the first-run wizard as any fresh install does; альтернатива: erase the city and the flags too (a complete fresh install: no city, the wizard again); цена ошибки: a person who wanted a complete start has to change the city by hand (one dialog); erasing it silently would cost the city of a person who only wanted the links reset, and the confirmation names links only.
   No in-page re-render and no announcement: the page reloads, so the new page starts at its top with no focus, and the screen reader reads the new page; accepted (Accepted exception 4).

7. **Slow and failing storage.** The removal has no timer of its own. A removal that answers after more than the 8 s of the status chip, with success, must give the same result as a fast one: the dialog stays open and busy for the whole time, no chip text appears, then the page reloads (AS-UGR-11). A removal that rejects (AS-UGR-12) shows the chip text above after it rejected, not before. A removal that is slow and then rejects shows the chip when it rejects and keeps the screen. A lock or a `remove` that never answers leaves the dialog busy for good (Accepted exception 13).

8. **Other tabs and a stale screen (closes Accepted exception 10 of run 50).**
   - A tab that shows a healthy grid and sees the meta go bad in another tab is left alone by a return, as `docs/tab-return-refresh.md` decision 5 step 3 says (zero change, nothing written, no message). Its next write is refused with `INVALID_WIDGETS_MESSAGE`; its reload shows the screen. A return never replaces a grid the person is looking at by the screen.
   - A tab that shows the screen is a locked-like state (`widgetsState` is `null`): a return does not re-read it. The screen therefore stays until a reload or a press of Reset grid, even when another tab has reset or fixed the data meanwhile. Ruling (марафон, на проверку Киру): the screen is not refreshed by a return; the guard of decision 6 makes the stale button safe (it erases nothing that is readable, and reloads to show the real state); альтернатива: let the return re-read the meta and draw the grid when it became readable (the decision of `refreshDeskOnReturn` would have to treat the screen as drawn and the render would have to drop the flag), also replacing the screen by the grid without a press; цена ошибки: with the chosen rule a stale screen can offer a reset that does nothing and shows the real grid after one extra confirm; with the alternative `refreshDeskOnReturn`, which needed several spec rounds in run 50, grows a branch for a state that almost no real user can reach.
   - Two screens pressed at once: the lock serializes them; the second gets the lock right after the first one's `remove` and finds no unreadable meta (it is `missing`, or `valid` if something wrote a grid meanwhile), so it erases nothing and reloads (AS-UGR-13).

The confirm dialog follows the focus rules of every desktop dialog (focus Cancel, Tab cycles Cancel and Reset grid, Escape closes). `p[role=alert]` is the one live announcement of the screen (as for `newer`); nothing is added to `#desktop-live`.

## Accepted exceptions

1. **Item 44 stays as decided by Kier:** two fresh installs before Chrome Sync delivers the other's keys can write the six system keys to different rows, and a merge by key can tear the block apart. Not reproducible in the harness, hard to cure; with the ceiling the rows of two windows now differ only up to the ceiling row (row 4 for the default block in one row, row 3 for the two-row block), so the exposure is smaller than before. No work.
2. **Reset is cross-device.** The removal goes through Chrome Sync like every write, so the grid's keys disappear on the account's other devices too; they were unreadable here and can be readable only to an older build there. The confirmation names links only (Kier's words). The check and the removal are one read and one call, but not one atomic step: a readable meta that Chrome Sync brings in between the `get(null)` and the `remove` is erased with the rest (the lock is local to the extension's pages; nothing closes this window, and it is milliseconds long).
3. **The city and the first-run flags stay (Ruling in decision 6).** A person who expected a complete start finds the city set; a person who never finished onboarding on this profile meets the wizard.
4. **The reset reloads the page.** There is no in-page success state and no announcement: the new page starts with no focus and the screen reader reads it as a new page. A person who pressed Reset grid sees the page flash once.
5. **A stale screen stays stale** until a reload or a press (Ruling in decision 8); a return does not draw the screen over a healthy grid either (decision 8, closes Accepted exception 10 of run 50).
6. **A slow second click is a new click.** After 300 ms a single click (`detail` 1) on a button of the dialog is a decision. The second click of a double click is caught at any time by `detail > 1` as long as the operating system counts it as one (its interval, about 500 ms by default); a click after that interval is a deliberate one.
7. **A window that is too low for the block.** The default block needs 5 rows (424 px at 601 px wide and more, 376 px at 361 to 600, 320 px up to 360) and an island needs 6 rows (504 px at 1280) or, with 5 rows of its own, 7 rows (584 px); a lower window shows a block stored at its ceiling only by scrolling; a profile written in a window of 400 px keeps its own lower row; a block already stored at row 5 or lower stays there (a stored `y` is never recomputed).
8. **A tall window shows the block above the middle** by the owner's decision (the cap), and the island too (Ruling in decision 2).
9. **Items that are unreadable one by one** (a valid meta whose item has bad fields) are left out of the read as before and stay in storage; no screen, no reset for them (Non-goals). The same holds for a second silent door: `getState()` of a readable meta returns the empty initial state when the assembled set fails `isWidgetsStateWith` (a type limit exceeded, a duplicate `id`; `src/widgetsStore.js`); the writes of Quiet Tab never produce such a set, a Chrome Sync merge can; the result is the empty grid with no text of L1-02, and it stays out of this run.
10. **A stored `tileSize` stays in a profile as ignored data**; an item that carried `tileSize: "huge"` and was invisible before is visible now.
11. **`INVALID_WIDGETS_MESSAGE` is not reworded** ("Reload this tab" is now true advice).
12. **A failure of the first read and of every later read** (storage down) is the existing `favoritesError` text, not the screen. A `newer` meta that the early read missed (the first read failed, then `ensureWidgetsLayout` reports `newer`) is still never written (`assertWritable`), and only its read-only message is not shown on that boot: `docs/remove-migrations.md` decision 6(a), unchanged here; AS-UGR-16 covers the same failed early read for `invalid` only.
13. **A lock or a `remove` that never answers** (the browser's lock manager or storage hangs) leaves the dialog busy for good: Cancel, Escape and the backdrop are ignored while `favoritesBusy`, there is no timer of its own and no visible waiting look (the delete confirm has none); the way out is a reload (F5). Chosen over a timer or a waiting state, which no other confirm dialog has.
14. **Below 328 px the two buttons of the dialog are not 50/50** (`Reset grid` takes 125 px at 320 px and Cancel 113): the one-line label needs more than half of the footer (decision 5).
15. **A press on the screen's button within 300 ms after the dialog closed by Cancel, Escape or the backdrop is swallowed** (`armPostCloseClickGuard()`, as after the wizard and the city modal); a deliberate press waits 300 ms.

## Non-goals

- Resetting or erasing for a `newer` meta; any automatic reset; a backup, export or undo of the erased grid; a partial recovery of single items; repairing a meta.
- Erasing the city, the weather cache, the first-run flags, the legacy favorites keys or any key that is not `quietTabWidgetsMeta` or `quietTabWidget:<id>`.
- Re-reading the grid on a return for the unreadable cases, drawing the screen on a return, any change to `refreshDeskOnReturn`, `healClearedDesk`, `recoverFromGoneLink` (run 50 and runs 29 to 31 stay).
- Chrome Sync partial arrival and the item 44 cure (so the "Sync edge cases" that `docs/tab-return-refresh.md` handed to this run are decided: accepted as they are); recomputing a stored row; any change to `placeNew`, the self-heal row, the drag drop area, the weather tiles or the wizard.
- New storage keys or formats, new permissions, new requests, a new setting; changes to `docs/privacy.md` or `store/`.
- `clearState` (test-only) and the other dead exports of the store; the `tileSize` mentions of historical specs beyond the notes of decision 10.
- A timer, a visible waiting state or a cancel for a removal that does not answer (Accepted exception 13).
