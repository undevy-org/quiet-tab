## Terms

- **`RUN59_BASE`**: `aaf24fa4df70b289cafc172f582569eccebff3a6`, the head of `main` when this spec was written (run 58 merged).
- **A render**: `renderFavorites()`, which ends in `renderDesktop()`; it recreates every tile node.
- **A started drag**: `dragSession !== null && dragSession.started === true` (the pointer moved 6 px or more). A press below that threshold is a tap, not a drag.
- **The drag grid**: the grid while a drag runs: `--rows` is `dragSession.rows` and `data-dragging="true"` (`applyDragVisuals`); it is as high as the allowed drop area, so it is taller than the grid at rest when the dragged tile is not alone in the lowest row.
- **Keyboard focus**: the focused tile matches `:focus-visible`.
- **The tooltip layer**: `#tooltip` (`showTooltipFor`, `hideTooltip`, `tooltipTrigger`).
- **An attempt**: the single in-flight weather request path of `retryWeather` (manual or automatic); **the load at open** is `startWeather`. Both own `weatherLoadInFlight` and a `weatherGeneration` value.
- **The ensure chip**: `#desktop-status` showing `ENSURE_FAILED_MESSAGE`, set persistent at load when `ensureWidgetsLayout` threw (`widgetsEnsureFailed`).
- **The second press of a swap**: a pointer press on the Add menu at most 500 ms after, and at most 4 px from, the click that moved it from the first level to the second (`Add weather tile…`).
- **The post-close guard**: `armPostCloseClickGuard()`, `POST_CLOSE_CLICK_GUARD_MS` (300 ms): a pointer `click` (`detail > 0`) is swallowed and the default of a `mousedown` is cancelled while it runs; `backgroundPressed` respects it (`postCloseGuardActive()`).

## Rulings

Each Ruling is the author's decision with the alternative not taken and the price of being wrong. They go to Kier in the final report of the run (not into the backlog).

1. **Ruling (134): the render keeps the drag height.** Taken: while a drag has started, `renderDesktop` gives the new grid the drag grid's height before it replaces the old one (decision 1). Not taken: running the page-height hold (`dragHoldPx`) during a drag, which needs a second set of rules for a state that has no settling. Price of a mistake: a drop after such a render lands one row off for a tile whose outline was valid; the outline and the write still agree (the outline is redrawn by `reattachDrag`).
2. **Ruling (154 (1)): one replay, by generation.** Taken: remember one `online` per attempt, replay it once after that attempt failed, and forget it when another attempt superseded the first (decision 2). Not taken: replay after success too (nothing to do, `weatherRefreshDue` is false) or a retry loop. Price: one extra request to Open-Meteo for the stored city after a failed attempt, only while a weather tile is shown and the tab is visible.
3. **Ruling (154 (2) and (4)): the ensure retry is silent at every heal site.** Taken: the same rule at the three heal sites, not only on a tab return (decision 3). Not taken: the return path only (the false "reset in another tab" would stay reachable through Add link and a gone-link recovery after the ensure failed). Price: a person whose storage another tab really cleared while the ensure chip was up would not see the reset notice; the grid is healed and drawn either way.
4. **Ruling (157): align the jiggle to the document timeline.** Taken: after each render in edit mode the new tile animations get `startTime = 0` (decision 5). Not taken: a CSS custom property holding the phase (it is up to one frame off), or leaving the jump (it is a polish row). Price: the animation start is overridden by script; if the browser refuses the assignment the tiles keep their own phase (the old behaviour).
5. **Ruling (159): the tooltip comes back for keyboard focus only.** Taken (decision 6). Not taken: also restoring a hover tooltip (hover returns by itself on the next pointer move, `AS-TRR-08`). Price: a hover tooltip that a render closed stays closed until the pointer moves.
6. **Ruling (148, keyboard scroll): hide, not follow.** Taken: any document scroll outside the 250 ms after a focus hides the tooltip (decision 7). Not taken: repositioning the tooltip on scroll (new behaviour, a per-frame layout read). Price: a keyboard user who scrolls with the arrow keys loses the tooltip and gets it back by moving focus away and onto the tile again.
7. **Ruling (148, S3-01): shrink the footer buttons, no Accepted exception.** Taken: `min-width: 0` on the 50/50 footer buttons (decision 8). Not taken: keeping the 2.67 and 3.38 px as an Accepted exception. Price: at a font above 24 px the label and icon overflow the button's padding (they are inside the border at 24 px, outside it from about 26 px, which Kier put outside the project).
8. **Ruling (148, L2-02): a smaller option padding keyed to the browser's font size.** Taken: `padding` 4 px (from 5 px) in a media query on `rem` (decision 9). Not taken: a container query on the group (more CSS for a row of numbers), or an Accepted exception. Price: at an enlarged font in a window of 336 px or less the three-option groups lose 1 px of side padding each, invisible at the default font.
9. **Ruling (170 (2)): the alert is spoken once.** Taken: `role` and `aria-live` come off the screen's paragraph when the Reset grid dialog first opens (decision 10). Not taken: keeping the alert (the repeated announcement stays), or a polite region. Price: a screen reader user who comes back to this page after switching tabs does not hear the message again; the text stays in the page and the Reset grid button stays the one tab stop.
10. **Ruling (182): cancel the focus move of a press, not the click.** Taken: a `mousedown` on the backdrop or on a non-control part of a dialog in the first 300 ms of its life has its default cancelled (decision 11). Not taken: arming the post-close guard at open (run 57 refused it: it would swallow a deliberate click inside the new dialog). Price: none visible; the clicks that the backdrop already ignored stay ignored.
11. **Ruling (183): double press by time and distance.** Taken: 500 ms and 4 px as the definition of the second press of a swap (decision 12). Not taken: a 300 ms window over all presses (swallows the deliberate quick pick of the first second-level item, `AS-WTA-06`), a taller second-level box (draws an empty box for one hidden metric). Price: Kier's variant A cost, plus a third press in the same place is also lost; a double click slower than 500 ms behaves as today.
12. **Ruling (184): `aria-invalid` only for an error about the typed value.** Taken: invalid for the empty field and for a city that was not found, `aria-describedby` for every shown error (decision 13). Not taken: invalid for every error (a network failure says nothing about the value). Price: a screen reader says "invalid entry" for a not-found city and not for a network failure.

## Decisions

1. **A render during a drag keeps the drag height (134).** While a drag has started, `renderDesktop` sets `--rows` of the new grid to `dragSession.rows` (and `data-dragging="true"`) before it replaces the old grid, so the document is as high after the render as before it and the browser has nothing to clamp. `reattachDrag` still picks up the dragged tile and redraws it and the outline, as today. Nothing else changes: with no started drag (a tap, no drag, a drag that ended) the render is exactly today's, and `dragHoldPx` and `dragSettling` are not read or written by this rule. The effect: `scrollY` does not change and no tile moves under the pointer because of such a render.
2. **An `online` during an attempt is remembered once (154 (1)).** When `onBrowserOnline` runs with the tab visible and a weather tile shown, and `retryWeather` returns because `weatherLoadInFlight` is set, the event is remembered with the current `weatherGeneration` (one value, overwritten by a later `online`). When that attempt or the load at open ends as the current generation, after its result is applied and rendered, a remembered value equal to its generation is consumed and one `retryWeather({ automatic: true, ignorePause: true })` is called; the replay first checks what its two callers check (`document.visibilityState === "visible"`, as `onBrowserOnline`; `navigator.onLine !== false`, as `onTabVisibilityChange`), and `retryWeather`'s own guards decide the rest (a shown tile, `weatherRefreshDue`), so after a success nothing starts. A remembered value of another generation (a manual press, a city change or a heal superseded the attempt) is dropped. The value is consumed on use: a failing replay never starts another; only a new `online` event can. The tab-return path (`onTabVisibilityChange`) is unchanged and remembers nothing.
3. **A heal after a failed ensure is silent, and a healthy re-read clears the chip (154 (2) and (4)).** While `widgetsEnsureFailed` is true, a heal that succeeds (`healClearedDesk` returns `healed`) is this tab's own retry of the ensure that failed at load, not another tab's reset: no `LAYOUT_RESET_MESSAGE`; `widgetsEnsureFailed` becomes false and, if `#desktop-status` shows the ensure chip (persistent, text `ENSURE_FAILED_MESSAGE`), it is hidden. This holds at the three heal sites (`healReadDesk` on a return, `runDesktopMutation`, `recoverFromGoneLink`); a failed heal is unchanged (`LAYOUT_RESTORE_FAILED_MESSAGE`, the Reload chip). On a return, a re-read state that has all six system tiles (the four `weather:` metrics, `chrome:settings`, `chrome:add`: the ids of `ENSURED_IDS`, which is exported from `widgetsStore.js` or mirrored by a pure `hasEverySystemTile(items)` there) clears the flag and the ensure chip even when it equals the shown state (the clear happens before the equal check, so `#favorites` is not touched); a re-read state that has Settings but lacks another system tile leaves the chip, the claim "couldn't add the tiles" still being true. No new text.
4. **The Edit weather city row follows an attempt (154 (3)).** After an attempt, manual or automatic, assigns a new `weatherLocation` (the replaced branch) or clears it (the `gone` branch) while an Edit weather dialog is open, `syncWeatherDialogCity()` runs. It returns at once, writing nothing, when the label it would write equals the one shown (the value, the hint and `aria-labelledby` unchanged), so a screen reader is not told the row again for no change. Focus, the typed fields and the rest of the dialog are untouched. With no change of the city the dialog is not touched at all (an equal label).
5. **The jiggle keeps its phase across renders (157).** In edit mode, at the end of `renderDesktop` (after the grid is attached, before any await), the animations named `tile-jiggle` of the new grid's tiles get `startTime = 0` (the document timeline origin). Every tile's phase is then a function of the clock only (`(timeline.currentTime - delay) mod 280 ms`, the odd/even stagger of -0.14 s kept), so a render does not restart it and a tile created by a render joins the same phase. Only `tile-jiggle` is touched (the `tile-added` ring keeps its own start). With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` there are no animations and nothing is called. Mechanics are the implementer's choice inside this rule; the acceptance is AS-BC-03.
6. **The tooltip comes back for keyboard focus after a render (159).** `renderDesktop` remembers, before its first `hideTooltip()`, the widget id of `tooltipTrigger` when the tooltip is shown for a tile that is the focused element and matches `:focus-visible`. After `restoreFocus`, if the focused element is that tile's new node (same `data-widget-id`, still `:focus-visible`) and the layers allow a tooltip (normal mode, no drag), `showTooltipFor` runs for it and `ignoreScrollUntil` is set 250 ms ahead (as `focusin` does, because the render may scroll). The text is the new node's. A tooltip that was open for the pointer is not restored (the pointer opens it again on the next move); a tile that lost focus, a dialog that took it, edit mode and a drag restore nothing. This is general for every render (weather on load, resize, a manual or automatic attempt, a re-read). The re-creation of the focused node itself is not touched.
7. **A document scroll hides the tooltip (148, keyboard scroll).** `onWindowScroll` (the passive `window` listener) first does what the `#favorites` capture listener does: when `performance.now() >= ignoreScrollUntil`, `hideTooltip()`. The 250 ms window after a focus (the browser scrolling the focused tile into view) and the one set by decision 6 keep the tooltip. The existing `wheel` and `touchmove` listeners and the `#favorites` scroll listener stay. A hidden tooltip is shown again by the existing triggers (a pointer over, a new keyboard focus).
8. **The 50/50 footer buttons may shrink (148, S3-01).** `min-width: 0` on `.favorite-form__footer .button`, `.city-modal__actions .button` and the wizard's footer buttons (the same `flex: 1` pairs), so two buttons of `flex: 1` are equal halves at every font size and never wider than their half. At 16 px and 20 px nothing changes (the content fits, the halves are what they were). The acceptance is in AS-BC-14; the mechanism is the one-line recommendation, to be confirmed by P8.
9. **A narrower option padding when the font is enlarged and the window is narrow (148, L2-02).** In `src/surfaces.css`, in a media query `(max-width: 14rem)` (the browser's default font size, as the query at the row stacking; at the default 16 px it is 224 px and never matches, at 24 px it is 336 px), `.desktop-dialog .segmented__option` gets `padding: 0 4px` (from `0 5px`). Arithmetic from the cited numbers: the last label's slack is `23.6 - 5p` at the 17 px bar, so `p` of 4 gives 3.6 px; measure and take the smallest reduction that meets AS-BC-15 (3 px if 4 px is not enough). Nothing changes at 16 px and 20 px. The same rule covers every `.segmented` group of the dialogs (Icon, Color, Size, On tile, City label).
10. **The screen's alert is spoken once (170 (2)).** When the Reset grid dialog (`confirm-reset-grid`) first opens, `role` and `aria-live` come off the `p` of `.grid-unreadable` (the text stays); a module flag remembers that, so a screen built later in the same page load (the node is recreated by a render) is built without them. At first load the paragraph is still `role="alert"`, so the message is announced once as before. The Reset grid button, the dialog, the failure chip (`#desktop-status`, its own alert) and the `newer` screen's alert are unchanged.
11. **A press right after a dialog opened does not move the focus (182).** `openDesktopDialog` adds a `mousedown` listener on the backdrop and on the dialog root: within `DIALOG_BACKDROP_GUARD_MS` (300 ms) of the open, when the target is the backdrop or lies in the dialog but not inside an `input`, `button`, `label`, `select`, `textarea` or `a`, `preventDefault()` is called (the focus stays where `openDesktopDialog` put it). The backdrop's `click` guard, the Reset grid guard and every click handler are unchanged; no flag, timer or constant is added (`desktopDialogOpenedAt` and `DIALOG_BACKDROP_GUARD_MS` exist). After 300 ms a press on the backdrop moves the focus as today.
12. **The second press of a swap does nothing (183, Kier's variant A).** The click that moves the Add menu to its second level (`add-weather` in `onAddMenuClick`) records its point and `event.timeStamp` when `event.detail > 0` (a pointer; a keyboard activation records nothing). In the document `pointerdown` listener of the Add menu, before its other conditions, a press that falls within 500 ms and 4 px of the recorded one while the menu is open (`addMenuRoot`) calls `armPostCloseClickGuard()` and returns: the menu is not closed, nothing is restored, `addMenuDismissPress` is not set, and the guard swallows the `click` of that press, cancels its `mousedown` default and keeps `backgroundPressed` from leaving edit mode (the three Run 57 mechanisms, no new listener). The record is dropped when the menu closes or after 500 ms. Constants: `ADD_MENU_DOUBLE_PRESS_MS = 500`, `ADD_MENU_DOUBLE_PRESS_SLOP_PX = 4` (the browser's default double-click interval and a hand's tremor). A press elsewhere, or after 500 ms, behaves as today: a deliberate quick pick of another item (`AS-WTA-06`) and an outside press are unchanged. With three hidden metrics the second press lands on the second level's item and its click is swallowed (nothing restored, the second level stays open); with one hidden metric it lands outside the shorter menu and does not close it; with a link tile under the point its click does not open `edit-link`.
13. **The city field names its error (184).** The error slot `p[data-city-modal-error]` gets a stable `id` (one city form exists at a time). While the slot shows a message the input has `aria-describedby` equal to that id; when the slot is empty or hidden the attribute is removed. `aria-invalid="true"` is set only while the message is about the typed value: `Enter a city name` (the empty submit, in the city modal and on wizard step 1) and a city that was not found (`City "…" was not found`, kind `notFound`). Every other message (network, timeout, rate limit, unknown, a location lookup failure) is described but the field is not invalid. Both attributes are cleared by `clearCityError` (typing) and by every path that empties or hides the slot (`syncCityModal`, a new form). ## Accepted exceptions

1. **A deliberate quick second press is lost (183).** Kier's price of variant A: a press within 500 ms and 4 px of the click that moved the menu to its second level does nothing, so does a third press there; and the 300 ms post-close guard that the swallowed press arms also swallows any deliberate pointer press in those 300 ms (the same trade as the other guarded closes, `docs/wizard-trap-add-dblclick.md` Accepted exception 1). A double click slower than 500 ms behaves as before.
2. **A press does not move the focus in the first 300 ms of a dialog (182).** A press on the backdrop or on a non-control part of a dialog within 300 ms of its open leaves the focus where it was; clicks (the backdrop's own guard ignores them) and presses on controls are not touched.
3. **A hover tooltip is not restored (159).** After a render the tooltip of a keyboard-focused tile returns; one that was open for the pointer stays closed until the pointer moves (`AS-TRR-08`).
4. **A scroll hides the tooltip (148).** Keyboard, wheel and scrollbar scrolls hide it; it does not follow the tile.
5. **The alert is not repeated (170).** After the Reset grid dialog first opens, the screen's paragraph is plain text; a screen reader user who comes back to the page without a reload does not hear it again. A real screen reader was not run; the owner's VoiceOver smoke (roadmap, "Только Кир") is the check.
6. **The jiggle starts at an arbitrary phase when edit mode is entered (157).** It is a function of the clock, not of the moment of the click; invisible.
7. **A replay can come after a replay's own cause is gone (154 (1)).** The replay goes through the ordinary guards (`due`, a shown tile, a visible tab, online), so a state that changed meanwhile is respected; the only residue is one request for the stored city after a failure.
8. **Fonts above 24 px stay out (148).** The geometry fixes are measured to 24 px; 32 px and more is outside the project (Kier, 2026-11-15, rows 148 L3-01 and 170 (1)).
9. **The accessibility tree, not a speech output, is measured (170, 184).** `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, `role` and the computed description are asserted; what VoiceOver says is not.
